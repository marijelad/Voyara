Privacy for this portfolio demo.

A concise explanation of what the static demo stores locally.

Browser storage

Voyara stores theme preference, saved destinations and sample trip drafts in localStorage on your device. The included project does not send that data to a server.

Forms

Demo contact forms prevent submission and show an on-screen confirmation only. No message is transmitted.

Third-party services

The packaged project uses no required third-party analytics, ad networks, booking engines or tracking pixels.

Production use

If this concept is adapted into a real service, replace this page with a privacy notice that accurately reflects the production data flows, vendors and legal requirements.
