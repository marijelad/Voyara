Guides

Travel planning that leaves room to travel.

Six practical planning principles built into the Voyara interface.

01

Plan around anchors, not hours

Choose one important morning stop and one important afternoon stop. Everything else becomes optional rather than stressful.

02

Cluster by neighborhood

A good-looking itinerary can still be bad if it ignores travel time. Group nearby stops before adding more.

03

Protect the first evening

Arrival days are unreliable. Keep the first night light and close to where you are staying.

04

Budget with a buffer

Treat the buffer as part of the plan, not money you hope not to use. Transport changes and spontaneous finds happen.

05

Leave one open block

A trip needs at least one block that can absorb weather changes, tiredness, a new recommendation or simply doing nothing.

06

Verify before travel

Opening hours, transit schedules, ticket rules and local entry requirements change. Check official sources close to departure.
